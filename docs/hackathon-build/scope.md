# Driftline winning scope

## Goal

Win the Google All Things Agentic Hackathon with the clearest and most credible
Taskmaster proof of autonomous action: one monitored commercial promise changes,
Driftline verifies the evidence, maps downstream work, pauses at a deterministic
human gate, executes one least-privilege owner action, and reverses it without
losing provenance.

## Primary user

A product-marketing or RevOps operator at a B2B software company who owns the
gap between a public change and the internal promises, enablement, and work
items that must change because of it.

## Wow moment

In one uninterrupted proof-of-action sequence, a live Gemini 3.5 Flash + Google
ADK workflow reaches the human gate, a signed operator approves an evidence-bound
Jira task, retry reuses the same marker, and **Reopen decision** reverses only
Driftline-owned Jira state while the audit ledger remains append-only.

## Time budget

Eight calendar days remain from the August 23 planning checkpoint to the August
31 deadline. Engineering is substantially complete. Remaining time is reserved
for release verification, narrative compression, architecture and demo assets,
Devpost registration/project setup, and a final compliant submission review.

## What we are shipping

1. Public, credential-free packet-safe judge lane on Cloud Run.
2. Live Gemini/ADK analysis with Firestore state and Cloud Tasks execution.
3. Bounded source registry, immutable evidence hashes, and impact mapping.
4. Deterministic approval policy outside model authority.
5. Signed tenant lane with one real, idempotent, reversible Jira action.
6. A four-minute-or-shorter proof-of-action video and concise Devpost packet.
7. Reproducible repository, architecture diagram, verification commands, and
   explicit limitations.

## Explicit cuts

- No Fortified Enterprise Fleet claim. Driftline is entered as Taskmaster.
- No additional connector expansion unless a release gate fails.
- No speculative customer ROI, revenue, retention, or willingness-to-pay claim.
- No arbitrary web crawling or anonymous external writes.
- No additional Google model added merely to chase bonus points.
- No UI panel expansion. The current product already contains more than enough
  proof; the submission must make the core journey easier to understand.

## Definition of done

The exact candidate passes all local and hosted gates; the public URL remains
free and accessible; the Devpost draft answers every official field truthfully;
the architecture file is upload-ready; the public video is no longer than four
minutes and visibly proves Google Cloud; and final submission occurs only after
Mike explicitly confirms the completed packet.
