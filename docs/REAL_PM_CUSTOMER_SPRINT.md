# Real PM validation and customer sprint

Status: local preparation only. This document does not authorize outreach,
publication, registration, deployment, or a customer claim.

## Brutal commercial answer

A genuine independent PM session before the deadline is feasible. A paid
customer in the same window is possible but not a responsible forecast. A
participant becomes a customer only after payment is received or a paid pilot
is signed; a call, compliment, email reply, or free trial is validation, not a
customer.

The fastest honest path is a narrow founder-led service, not a self-serve SaaS
sale: bring one consequential decision whose evidence may change, leave with a
cited and reversible decision brief, then review the same decision after its
pre-agreed measurement window.

## Pain worth targeting

Current first-party product research points to four connected problems:

- customer evidence already lives across support, sales, analytics, research,
  and delivery tools, and the hard part is organizing it into action;
- disconnected discovery makes prioritization look ad hoc or driven by the
  loudest stakeholder;
- teams want feedback and reasoning connected to delivery so priorities can be
  revisited continuously rather than handed off once;
- budget pressure and data silos are driving tool consolidation, so a new tool
  must prove a distinct outcome rather than add another inbox.

## Smallest qualified participant

Prioritize a Head or VP of Product, Product Operations lead, fractional product
leader, product-marketing lead, or founder-PM at a pre-seed through Series B
B2B software company. Qualify the session only when all of these are true:

1. They own or materially recommend a rollout, roadmap, pricing, packaging,
   positioning, platform, segment, or resource-allocation decision due within
   30 days.
2. At least two plausible options have meaningful downside.
3. They can provide three or more safely redacted evidence inputs without raw
   customer data, credentials, or private identifiers.
4. They agree to a 3-, 7-, 14-, or 30-day outcome review.
5. They understand that the agent recommends while a named human authorizes,
   and that the public lane makes no external customer-system writes.

Disqualify generic curiosity, retrospective toy examples, decisions with only
one viable answer, or anyone unable to return for an outcome check.

## The offer

**Decision-drift session:** Bring one real product decision due within 30 days.
In 30 minutes, Driftline will organize the competing evidence, preserve the
dissent, compare reversible options, and bind the selected path to a measurable
review. No credentials or raw customer records are required.

The first session can be a no-cost qualification pilot. Ask for a paid second
decision only after the participant sees the first result. Do not use a token
payment, refundable deposit, or friendly transfer merely to manufacture a
customer label for judges.

## Three-day founder-led funnel

These are activity targets, not predicted results.

| Window | Target | Evidence produced |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Day 1 | 20 warm or one-hop candidates reviewed | public role/company fit and reason for fit |
| Day 1 | 10 individually approved invitations | sent-message receipt, never a bulk blast |
| Day 2 | 3 qualified replies | qualification answers and scheduled time |
| Day 2–3 | 1–3 real-decision sessions | one anonymized record per session |
| Day 3+ | 1 outcome review or paid-next-decision ask | measured verdict or commercial status |

Warm paths should come first: former colleagues, founders in the entrant's
network, fractional product leaders, and portfolio/community operators who can
bring a live decision. Cold outreach is a lower-probability backup because the
deadline is close and a real decision requires trust.

Warm does not mean "an email thread exists." Exclude recruiting and interview
threads, investor or fundraising conversations, sensitive professional
relationships, and any relationship where the original context would make a
pilot request feel coercive or opportunistic. Use an organizer-mediated opt-in
introduction when no clean direct relationship is established.

## What Codex can do continuously

An ongoing read-only task can:

1. find public evidence of likely candidates and current product-change
   triggers;
2. score each candidate against the qualification gate;
3. deduplicate the list and retain the public source URL;
4. draft a specific invitation based on the public trigger;
5. prepare the session packet and validate an anonymized result with
   `scripts/summarize_real_pm_pilot.py`;
6. report the funnel and blockers every morning.

It should never send a message, submit a form, book a meeting, publish a claim,
or label someone a customer without an explicit human-approved action. The
human must also supply or authorize access to a warm network; public research
alone cannot reliably identify trust relationships.

## Evidence custody

Copy `docs/validation/real-pm-pilot-template.json` for each session. Store no
name, company name, email, transcript, raw note, CRM record, or credential in
the record. The validator fails closed on weak qualification, premature
outcomes, unsupported public claims, citation errors, misunderstood authority,
and customer labels without payment or a signed paid commitment.

One well-qualified session can support a bounded independent-PM statement.
Three genuine sessions are materially stronger. Neither guarantees a judge
score, and no honest work can guarantee 100/100.
