complete the workflow, and voluntarily make a next commitment such as a second
decision or outcome follow-up. Payment to a participant must never be described
as revenue.

## Qualification gate for every route

Accept a participant only when all five are true:

1. They personally recommend, approve, or are accountable for a product
   decision that must be made by **September 25, 2026**.
2. Two real options exist and at least one stakeholder could reasonably overturn
   the current preference.
3. A wrong choice has a concrete cost: revenue, activation, retention, delivery,
   customer trust, compliance, or team capacity.
4. They can safely redact the evidence and may discuss it without violating an
   employer, client, or confidentiality obligation.
5. They will state their current choice and confidence **before** seeing
   Driftline, then say what changed, what did not, and the first reason they
   would reject the result.

Reject generic demos, retrospective stories, job-seeking conversations, advice
calls, and participants who cannot name a real deadline. Publication consent is
separate from participation; no name, company, quote, recording, or result may
be published without explicit later permission.
