Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Driftline handles information when you use the Driftline demonstration console, evaluation environment, or a connector that you intentionally authorize. Driftline is a hackathon project operated by Mike Yerke. Questions can be sent to mikeyerke@gmail.com.

Short version: the public demonstration uses synthetic workflow data and explicitly allowlisted public sources. It is not connected to a customer’s private systems by default, and it does not sell personal information.

Information we handle

  • Demo and workflow data. The console records bounded job, source-observation, evidence, decision, action-item, and audit-event data needed to run and explain a workflow. The public demo is designed to use synthetic data.
  • Public source content. Driftline may fetch an explicitly allowlisted public URL or a pinned demonstration fixture to detect a change. Source snapshots are limited in size and scope; Driftline is not an unrestricted web crawler.
  • Operator identity and configuration. If an operator uses an authenticated connector or operator-only feature, Driftline may process the operator email/subject, tenant identifier, connector scope, and health status needed to enforce access controls. Connector secrets are kept in the isolated runtime secret store and are not returned to the browser.
  • Technical information. Cloud Run and related infrastructure may produce normal security and reliability logs such as timestamps, request paths, error details, and approximate network information.

How information is used

Driftline uses information to detect and explain changes, map affected work, prepare evidence-bound packets, enforce deterministic approval rules, provide connector handoffs that an operator has enabled, secure the service, troubleshoot failures, and measure the reliability of a pilot. Driftline does not use the public demo to make autonomous customer-facing decisions.

Connected services

Connectors are opt-in and scoped to the tenant, project, space, channel, repository, or read-only Salesforce objects that the operator configures. The connected service may retain its own copy of data under its own policy. Driftline does not claim access to a connector unless the connection has been directly authenticated and health-checked.

Retention and deletion

The isolated deployment uses bounded retention for workflow, job, outcome, and source-history records; its default retention window is 30 days and may be changed by an authorized operator. Connector secrets are stored separately from workflow records. External systems retain objects created there according to their own policies. To ask about deletion of Driftline-held information or to report a privacy concern, contact mikeyerke@gmail.com with enough detail to identify the relevant record.

Security

Driftline uses HTTPS in the deployed environment, least-privilege connector boundaries, server-side secret handling, bounded requests, and deterministic human approval for high-risk actions. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so do not submit passwords, payment information, API tokens, private keys, or other secrets through the public demo.

Children and sensitive data

Driftline is not directed to children. Do not submit sensitive personal information or private customer data to the public demonstration.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Driftline moves from a hackathon demonstration toward a pilot. The “Last updated” date above will change when material revisions are made.
