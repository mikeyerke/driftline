# Driftline paid independent-PM recruit packet

Status: local launch draft only. Do not create an account, launch a study,
message or schedule a participant, or spend money without Mike approving the
provider, exact checkout total, incentive, dates, and final text.

## Study listing

**Title:** Test one real product decision under conflicting evidence

**Format:** 30-minute private remote product-research session, with a short
3-, 7-, 14-, or 30-day outcome check only when that window is operationally
honest for the decision.

**Description:**

We are testing Driftline, a decision workflow for product leaders facing a
current contested commitment. Bring one real decision due within 30 days and
three or more safely redacted, non-confidential evidence inputs. You will state
your current choice before using the workflow, review every generated citation,
and identify what changed, what did not, and the first reason you would reject
the result. A negative result is useful. Do not share customer records,
personal data, credentials, privileged material, regulated data, or employer-
or client-confidential information.

This is compensated product research. Compensation is for the participant's
time, not an endorsement, purchase, customer commitment, or permission to
publish. No identity, company, quote, recording, or result will be published
without separate later consent.

## Targeting

- Senior Product Manager, Group Product Manager, Head/VP of Product, Product
  Operations leader, fractional product leader, or founder-PM.
- Currently responsible for B2B software, preferably pre-seed through Series B
  or a small portfolio where one person has direct decision authority.
- Must personally own, recommend, or be accountable for the submitted decision.
- Exclude anyone involved in building or judging Driftline, paid implementation
  work for Driftline, or a relationship that would make participation
  non-independent.

## Screener and automatic disposition

1. **Which best describes your current role?**
   - Accept only a target role above; manually review adjacent product roles.
2. **Are you currently responsible for a product decision that must be made in
   the next 30 days?**
   - Reject `no`.
3. **What date must the decision be made, and what makes that date real?**
   - Reject a date outside 30 days or an answer with no operational reason.
4. **What kind of decision is it?**
   - Accept rollout, roadmap, pricing, packaging, positioning, platform,
     segment, or resource allocation. Manually review other categories.
5. **How are you involved?**
   - Accept owner or material recommender. Reject observer/advisor-only unless
     they are directly accountable for the resulting recommendation.
6. **How many plausible options are genuinely still open? Briefly name them.**
   - Reject fewer than two or an already irreversible choice.
7. **What concrete downside could follow from choosing incorrectly?**
   - Accept a specific revenue, activation, retention, delivery, trust,
     compliance, or capacity cost. Reject generic “we may learn less.”
8. **Can you discuss the decision using only safely redacted,
   non-confidential information and at least three evidence inputs?**
   - Reject `no` or any need to upload raw customer, personal, regulated,
     credential, privileged, or contract-restricted data.
9. **Before seeing Driftline, will you state your current option and confidence
   from 1–7, then open and review every generated citation?**
   - Reject `no`.
10. **Could the decision produce an honest leading indicator in 3, 7, 14, or
    30 days, and are you willing to complete that brief follow-up?**
    - Reject `no`; do not promise a three-day outcome when the decision cannot
      produce one.
11. **Are you involved in building, funding, implementing, or judging
    Driftline, or do you have another conflict that should be disclosed?**
    - Reject build/judging/paid-implementation involvement. Manually review any
      other disclosed relationship and do not call it independent validation
      unless independence is defensible.

The final free-response answers must be manually reviewed. Panel verification
and a senior title do not substitute for a real decision, authority, or safe
evidence.

## Scheduling and incentive gate

- Offer the earliest viable sessions before the deadline, but schedule only
  after manual qualification.
- Launch one provider at a time. Do not pay duplicate panels to fill the same
  evidence gap.
- Review the live provider fee, participant incentive, cancellation terms, and
  total checkout before approval. Public pricing and matching times are vendor
  claims and may change.
- Record the final recruiting channel and participant incentive in
  `submission/PILOT_SESSION_WORKSHEET.md`.

## Session exit criteria

A completed session counts as qualified independent-PM evidence only if the
participant passes the screener, states the initial choice before Driftline,
reviews every citation, understands named-human authority and external-writes-
none, and completes the bounded session record. Use
`scripts/summarize_real_pm_pilot.py` to fail closed on the public statement.

Classification is cash-direction sensitive:

- Driftline pays participant: independent evaluation expense, not revenue.
- Participant voluntarily schedules a second live decision or follow-up:
  design-partner signal, not automatically a customer.
- Participant pays Driftline or signs the bounded paid-pilot commitment:
  customer evidence, subject to exact documentation.

Never ask a paid participant to purchase, endorse, or grant publication consent
as a condition of receiving the promised incentive.
