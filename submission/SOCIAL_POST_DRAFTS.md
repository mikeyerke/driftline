## LinkedIn

I built Driftline for the Google All Things Agentic Hackathon.

Product decisions rarely fail for lack of opinions. They fail when usage,
customer, strategy, and feasibility evidence conflict—and the assumptions
behind the decision disappear before anyone checks the outcome.

Driftline turns one contested commitment into an inspectable learning loop.
Five independent Google ADK specialists use Gemini 3.5 Flash on Vertex AI to
produce cited positions without erasing dissent. A named human chooses one
falsifiable experiment. Cloud Tasks then evaluates the committed measurement;
if the guardrail breaks, Driftline rolls back its bounded internal decision
state and reopens the same case as generation 2 with the original evidence,
approval, and result still attached.

The part I am proudest of is the authority boundary. The model can recommend,
but it cannot approve itself or bypass the evidence hash, risk threshold, and
rollback contract. The public judge path says exactly what changed:
**Driftline decision state only; external writes: none.**

Live demo: https://driftline-ops.web.app/
Code: https://github.com/mikeyerke/driftline
