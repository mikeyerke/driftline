## Commercial distinction

A product advisor who critiques Driftline is an expert evaluator, not a
customer. A community member who uses one current decision is a design partner,
not automatically a customer. Only money or a signed paid-pilot commitment
supports a customer claim.

The fastest credible judging result is one real-decision design partner with a
three-day leading-indicator review. The fastest credible commercial result is a
design partner who then schedules a price conversation or signs a bounded paid
pilot. Trying to force payment before the August 31 deadline is more likely to
reduce participation than strengthen the entry.

## The one ask that fits the deadline

> I am looking for one product leader with a real, safely redacted decision—not
> general feedback. In 30 private minutes we will compare Driftline with your
> current judgment, commit the primary metric and risk guardrail, and schedule a
> three-day leading-indicator check only if that window is operationally honest
> for your decision. Nothing is posted or quoted without separate permission.
> A negative result is useful. Would you test one live decision?

Do not promise a three-day result when the product decision cannot produce a
meaningful leading indicator that quickly. In that case, capture the initial
decision-quality evidence before judging and keep the outcome follow-up open.

## Conversion sequence

1. Qualify the live decision before showing the product.
2. Run the private 30-minute workflow and record the before/after evidence.
3. Ask for one costly next step: follow-up, teammate, introduction, or price
   conversation.
4. If the participant wants continued use, offer a narrowly scoped paid pilot;
   use `submission/PAID_DESIGN_PARTNER_OFFER.md` as the local draft and do not
   invent urgency, discounts, or a customer label.
5. Request anonymized publication consent separately from product participation
   and separately from any commercial agreement.

## Planning assumptions, not evidence

An in-person, topic-matched introduction is directionally more likely to produce
a qualified session than cold contact. A paying commitment within days remains
a low-probability upside. These are recruiting assumptions, not measured
Driftline conversion rates, and must never appear in the submission as results.
